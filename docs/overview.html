Voice-first helper for ASHA workers · Uttar Pradesh · EnVision '26

A.U.R.A.

After a home visit, the ASHA speaks one short note. A.U.R.A. turns it into a tidy visit record, she checks it once, and the app reuses it for her task list, her reports and the official forms. She never has to write the same thing twice.

She says (made-up example)

“आज सुनीता देवी के बच्चे का सातवें दिन का दौरा किया, वजन 2 किलो 800 ग्राम, बुखार नहीं, आँखों में मवाद नहीं।”

→

A.U.R.A. fills in

WhoSunita Devi's baby
VisitNewborn (HBNC), day 7
Weight2.8 kg
FeverNo
Eyes swollen / pusNo

The whole project on one page

Six parts. The left side is what the ASHA does with her note; the right side is what the note feeds and the rules A.U.R.A. keeps.

Her visit, step by step

Speak

Tap the big green button and say what happened. Hindi, English or a mix.

Check

The review screen shows every value next to the words she said. Anything doubtful is flagged.

Add or fix

Type a change, tap Yes/No, or speak again ("weight 3 kg"). Only what she says changes.

Confirm

One tap saves it. The recording is deleted. Next visits and vaccines appear on Today.

Reuse

Open the visit any time: see it in the official form order, fill what's still missing, copy or autofill.

What happens behind the button

The phone app talks to a small server running on a laptop. Nothing goes to any government system.

Phone

Records her voice (or takes typed text). If there's no connection, the note waits in an outbox and is sent later.

Laptop

Speech to text. Sarvam (cloud, good at Hindi and Hinglish) when a key is set, otherwise Vosk (offline, weaker).

Laptop

Find the facts. Fixed rules pull out who, which visit, weight, temperature, danger signs, vaccines, referral, next date, and the extra form questions. Each value must point to the exact words it came from.

Laptop

Double-check. Code checks ranges and dates, matches the name to her list of people, and leaves anything not said empty (never guessed).

ASHA

Reviews and confirms. Nothing is final until she taps save.

Laptop

Works out what's due from programme schedules (newborn, young child, vaccines), and builds reports and form views.

What's on each screen

Today

Visits and vaccines due, overdue first, grouped by status or area. Unsent notes. Recent visits to reopen.

Note

Record, type or upload. A short guide of what to say for each visit type.

Review

Who, visit type, vitals, danger signs, services, referral, next dates. Speak again to add. Forms also ask card (optional).

New in v1.12

Saved visit

Opens from Today or People. View form, Edit note, Read aloud.

New in v1.12

View form

Her visit in the order of an official form. Still missing list: tap, type or say the answers, then save.

People

Add mothers, newborns, children. Each person's details and visits.

Reports

Monthly report and incentive claim-support sheet as PDF or Excel. Correction stats.

Settings

Language, server address, demo data, privacy notice, delete her data.

Official forms and autofill

Forms the app can lay out

  • HBNC home visit (newborn, 2014 format)
  • SSBSK newborn (Form 3)
  • SSBSK mother after delivery (Form 4)
  • SSBSK young child
  • RCH register, child tracking (CH-1, CH-2)

Questions a note rarely covers (baby kept warm? only breastfed? RCH ID?) are asked on review and listed as "still missing" on each form. Details about the child are saved on the person once.

How autofill works (demo stage)

  • She opens the other app or website and logs in herself.
  • She taps a field; Android offers "A.U.R.A.: fill N fields".
  • Fields are matched by their label. She checks each one.
  • Never reads or fills password, OTP, PIN or login fields.
  • Never presses Submit. Demos stop before it.

Practice targets are labelled SIMULATED. The government's permission is asked before real use.

Promises A.U.R.A. keeps

Two permissions onlyMicrophone and notifications.
No trackingNo location, contacts, calls, SMS, attendance or scoring.
Shows its sourceEvery value carries the words it came from.
Blank, not guessedIf she didn't say it, it stays empty.
She decidesNothing is saved or filled without her tap.
Advice onlySchedules are drafts until a clinician reviews them.

Where it stands (16 Nov 2026)

Working and checked

Words used here

ASHA
Accredited Social Health Activist, the village health worker who uses the app.
ANM
Auxiliary Nurse Midwife, her supervisor at the sub-centre.
HBNC / HBYC
Home-based care visits for newborns (first 42 days) and young children.
SSBSK
The 2026 newborn and child home-visit programme with its own forms.
RCH
The government register and portal for mothers and children; each has an RCH ID.
Autofill
Android filling a form in another app from a saved visit, after she taps.
SIMULATED
A practice stand-in for something real we don't have, such as a login or a portal.
