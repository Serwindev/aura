## 2. SSBSK Shaishav App reporting format — ASHA section (SSBSK 2026, Annexure 3, pp. 112–118) [S36]

The newer digital form. Visit columns: newborn D1 D3 D5 D7 D10 D14 D21 D28 D42; young child M3 M6 M9 M12 M18 M24 M30 M36.

- **Form 1 (general, autofilled from ABHA ID):** mobile, ABHA ID, mother/father/child name, address, discharge dates, SNCU discharge, cried at birth, first feed, breastfeeding start (< 1 h … > 24 h), immunisation (autofill from U-WIN), DOB, time, birth weight (g), stillbirth, MCP card, place and type of delivery, sex.
- **Availability:** newborn/child available? if not: not alive / admitted / visiting relatives / migrated; mother alive?
- **Form 2B at-risk newborn:** birth weight < 2500 g · preterm < 37 wk · breastmilk not initiated · SNCU/NICU discharge · visible birth defect · regained birth weight by day 14 · weight gain in two visits after day 14 · referral status.
- **Form 3 newborn (0–2 months):** temperature (°F); danger signs: moves only when stimulated · not able to feed · convulsion · difficulty breathing · fast breathing > 60/min · severe chest in-drawing · yellow palms and soles · blood in stool · axillary temperature ≥ 37.5 °C · < 36 °C; referral status; weight (grams); exclusive breastfeeding (every 2 h, at night, urine 6–8 times, weight gain) or mixed/top feed; breastfeeding pain; engorgement/cracked nipple; KMC for LBW/preterm; skin pustules; eye discharge; umbilical pus/bleeding; household profile; counselling done.
- **Form 4 mother (PNC):** temperature (°F); danger signs: fever · severe anaemia/bleeding (≥ 5 pads/day) · UTI (> 102 °F, burning) · vaginal infection (foul discharge, > 100 °F) · abdominal pain · neurological (blurred vision/convulsion/speaking abnormally) · oedema · difficulty breathing · jaundice · incontinence; diet (3 meals + 2 snacks); hand washing; IFA & calcium; spacing method; distress (2 questions).
- **Form 5 at-risk young child (2–36 months):** weight (kg), length/height (cm), underweight/SAM/MAM, NRC discharge, recurrent illness, developmental delay, referral to NRC.
- **Form 6 young child:** temperature (°F); danger signs: not able to feed/drink · vomits everything · lethargy/unconscious · convulsions · difficulty breathing · fast breathing (2–12 mo > 50/min; 12 mo–5 yr > 40/min) · severe chest in-drawing · fever > 7 days without a doctor · diarrhoea > 14 days / dehydration · blood in stool; breastfeeding continued; complementary feeding by age; birth defect follow-up; deworming (> 1 yr); Vitamin A; IFA syrup; ORS at home; ECD and safety questions; household profile; counselling done.

## 3. UP RCH register — Section III, Tracking of Children (ANM's register) [S39]

CH-1: serial no. · RCH ID of child · child name (unnamed: "Male/Female child of <mother>") · sex · mother's name · mother's RCH ID · address · DOB · birth weight (kg) · place of birth (PHC/CHC/DH/private/accredited private/sub-centre/home/other) · religion · caste.
CH-2: vaccine doses with dates (primary, boosters, Vitamin A).
CH-3: exclusive breastfeeding to 6 months · complementary feeding after 6 months (else age started) · at 9–12 and 16–24 months: date, weight (kg), diarrhoea in last 15 days (ORS given?), pneumonia (antibiotics given?).

## 4. What this means for A.U.R.A.

1. The form's temperature is **°F**; the app stores °C. The form view shows both.
2. Questions the app cannot answer are shown blank: she fills them from her own check. **Not mentioned never becomes "No".**
3. Gaps worth adding next (ask first): mother's temperature, baby alive/available, eyes pus, cry weak, limbs limp, vomiting/distended abdomen, mother referral, number of pads.
4. SSBSK changes the schedule (see `context.md` §8.1/8.2, decision D-32).
5. HBYC guideline and job aid could not be downloaded (site timed out); the young-child list above comes from SSBSK Form 6 instead.
